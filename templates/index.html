THE RULES

How to play

Each day, a Letterboxd user's four favorite films are selected. Three posters are visible and one is hidden.

Use the clues to identify the missing film. Clues reduce your score, and every incorrect guess costs another 10 points.

If your score reaches zero, you can reveal the answer. You can also continue guessing while at zero.
